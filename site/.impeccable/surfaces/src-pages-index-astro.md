Scope: rofit.site homepage (Persuade) and the shared shell every site page inherits. Visitor: Roblox players (often teens) arriving from shared links, half on phones; plus cautious parents/techies checking privacy. Action: install (load unpacked today, store listings not live) or try the live demo. Proof: real public outfits from the dated snapshot, privacy claims generated from code. Constraints: no invented prices, rarity, users or endorsements; non-affiliation note; no Roblox chrome.

## Direction contract
THESIS: RoFit is a fashion editor for Roblox wardrobes: the page is a "get the look" spread where a real saved outfit is the cover and every piece is called out with what Roblox actually knows about it. Refuses the split SaaS hero (copy left, screenshot right, gradient behind).
OWN-WORLD: coated near-white stock and ink black, one fluoro-pink spot colour printed as whole fields (cover, compare spread), never as glow. Anybody variable: ultra-condensed heavy caps for cover lines, extended for totals; Onest for reading. Hairline leader lines with dot terminals; collector cards (outfit render on stock, ruled stat strip, "No." + real outfit ID). Known values print solid; unknown values print as hairline outline or dashed rule.
STORY: understand in one viewport what RoFit shows (saved outfits, piece by piece, honest prices); believe it from real outfits and the code-derived privacy proof; act: install or try the real UI.
FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed spot-pink cover. Left third: giant condensed "GET THE LOOK." over a two-line deck and the two actions. Centre-right: one real outfit render at ~70vh with 4-5 leader-line callouts (item, price or "Off sale", creator). Top-right: collector number + owner + snapshot date. Bottom: look switcher (previous/next real outfits) that re-deals the render and redraws the callouts.
FORM: get-the-look spread fused with collector archive; my ranked pick (1 of 7, user-chosen over assigned collector binder); seed key d083cea9.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Cited changes after build (finish review round 1)
- Masthead: the cover carries a full-width wide "ROFIT" masthead (Anybody 150% width) that the render overlaps, with "GET THE LOOK." as the cover line beneath it. Reason: on a magazine cover the masthead is the title; it gives the brand recognition the narrow cover line alone lacked and is the device the footer repeats. The narrow cover line stays the h1.
- Issue line: collector number, name, owner and snapshot date sit top-left under the masthead (the top-right is occupied by the masthead's last letters and the cover-line column).
- Visibility labels are re-inked in ink / spot / dashed instead of the extension's green/amber, so the site keeps one spot colour.
- Compare: two printed cards face to face at rest; the wipe is the second mode (mirrors the extension's Side by side / Wipe control).
