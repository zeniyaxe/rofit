# Items for the owner / a lawyer before launch

The /privacy, /terms and /security pages are plain-language drafts that describe what the extension verifiably does (generated from its manifest). They are not legal advice. Decide or confirm:

1. **Operator identity and jurisdiction** (name, country) for Terms and Privacy. Currently "The RoFit project".
2. **Source licence.** None chosen; Terms say reuse is not licensed.
3. **Warranty/liability wording** in Terms, and governing law.
4. **Contact addresses** (`LINKS.supportEmail`, `LINKS.securityEmail` in `src/config.ts`). Never invent one.
5. **Roblox trademark use.** The site names Roblox only descriptively and carries a non-affiliation notice; Roblox's name/logo guidelines page was not readable during research (HTTP 403), so confirm against it. No Roblox logos or assets are used.
6. **Chrome Web Store 2026 disclosure rules** (data collection must be disclosed): RoFit collects nothing, so the listing should say so and link /privacy.
7. **Hosting logs.** Privacy says the host may keep ordinary server logs; confirm what the chosen host actually does.
8. **Children's privacy** wording.
